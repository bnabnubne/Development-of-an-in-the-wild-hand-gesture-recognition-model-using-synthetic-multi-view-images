Unified result notes, 2026-07-08

Project direction

The deployable pipeline is:

single RGB image
MediaPipe 3D hand skeleton
geometric normalization
single-view GRU inference

Synthetic Blender views are used during training. DrOh is used as external test data.

Submitted thesis result

Raw baseline:
Salux 96.85 percent
DrOh 77.93 percent

Raw skeleton with Blender8 consistency, lambda 0.3:
Salux 97.05 percent
DrOh 78.96 percent

The gain is 1.03 percentage points on DrOh.

Fitted-template results

Fitted-template experiments give high oracle numbers when the correct gesture template is known. They should not be reported as normal in-the-wild accuracy.

Main interpretation

The submitted result remains valid. Later checks show that most of the gain comes from supervised synthetic-view augmentation. The consistency term is useful in some schedules, but it should be compared with a matched training schedule.
